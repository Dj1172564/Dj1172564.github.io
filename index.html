For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Any recipe, as a timeline.

Recimama turns a recipe into a timeline. It works out which steps can happen at the same time, tells you what to do next and sets an alert for each step, so everything comes together when you want to eat.

Now on the App Store Free to download, with an optional subscription or one-time purchase.

1 One recipe

Cooking is a plan, not a list.

Each step knows how long it takes and whether it needs your hands, a watchful eye or nothing at all, so the water heats while the bacon fries. Recimama works out what has to wait for what and overlaps everything else, but never more than your kitchen can do at once, going by the burners and ovens you tell it you have.

In the app’s charts, a step’s color shows how much attention it needs:

  • Hands on
  • Keep an eye on it
  • Looks after itself

The chopping and grating come first, all of it before step 1, and they’re counted in the time. Change the servings, or switch between metric and imperial, and the amounts follow. Want to eat at a set time? Work back from it, and the recipe tells you when to start.

2 A whole meal

A whole meal, on one timeline.

Pick a main and a side or two and see them timed to finish together, with the sides slotted into the time the main is already taking, or read the whole meal as one recipe.

Recimama shows how long the meal takes cooked together against one dish after another, and when to start so it’s on the table on time.

Planning a meal and reading it as one recipe don’t need Pro. Cooking the dishes along together, on one clock, is part of Recimama Pro.

3 Cooking along

Put the phone down. It’ll tell you when.

Start a cook and the app keeps time: what to do now, what’s next, and how long is left.

  • An alert for each step, so you can put the phone down.
  • The timeline on your Lock Screen, counting down the step you’re on.
  • The step you’re on, on your Apple Watch, with one tap to say it’s done.
  • Turn the phone on its side for one big step at a time.
  • Or ask Siri, “What’s next in Recimama?”

Times are estimates. Recimama can’t see your pan, so always check that food is cooked through.

What needs you, on your wrist.

Start a cook on your phone and it appears on your Apple Watch: anything late first, then the step you’re on with its countdown, then what’s next and when it starts. Tap Done to tick the step off.

  • A late step counts up from when it should have finished.
  • “All steps” takes you back and forward through the recipe, one step at a time, starting at the one you’re on.
  • When the cook is over, the watch clears itself.
  • It comes with the iPhone app, and installs from the Watch app on your iPhone. It needs watchOS 11 or later.
  • No ads on the watch, and nothing there asks for Pro.
