# Report illegal content on Recimama

*Last updated: 29 September 2026*

Anyone can tell us about illegal content on Recimama. You don't need the app or an account, and it doesn't matter where you live. This page explains how to do it without the app. In the app, use **Report**.

## In the app

Open the recipe on Explore, or press and hold it in the list, and tap **Report**.

- For something illegal, choose **Illegal content — tell us which law**. Say which law, in which country, and what is illegal; the report can't be sent until you do.
- For an intimate image of you, choose **An intimate image of me shared without my consent**. Then send the email the app prepares, because we need to be able to contact you. It is written for you, with a line for your name and the statements UK law asks for: that you're the person in the image or acting for them, and that your report is made in good faith and is true.

An in-app report carries no way to contact you. If you'd like to hear what we decided, send the email the app offers to prepare. It goes from your own mail app, and your address is never saved with the report. For an illegal-content report the email also carries the good-faith statement a notice needs, for you to send or delete.

## Without the app: email us

Email **[rodrigobayapps@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigobayapps@gmail.com)** with "Illegal content report" in the subject. Please include what the EU Digital Services Act asks a report like this to contain:

1. **Why it's illegal.** Which law, in which country, and why you think the content breaks it. Explain it so that someone without legal training can see the problem.
2. **Where it is.** Explore can be seen only inside the Recimama app. So tell us:
   - the recipe's title
   - the cook's name as Explore shows it
   - roughly when you saw it
   - if you have one, a screenshot (never of child sexual abuse material: see below)
3. **Your name and email address.** You don't have to give them if you're reporting child sexual abuse material.
4. **A statement** that you believe, in good faith, that the information and allegations in your report are accurate and complete.

If something is missing we'll still read your report, and we'll ask for what we need.

## Urgent and special cases

- **Someone is in immediate danger.** Call the police first: 911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU.
- **Child sexual abuse material.**
  - Don't download, screenshot or forward it.
  - Report it to NCMEC's CyberTipline at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org/), or in the UK to the Internet Watch Foundation at [report.iwf.org.uk](https://report.iwf.org.uk/).
  - Then email us where it is: the recipe's title, the cook's name and when you saw it. You don't have to give your name.
  - We take it down, report it to NCMEC, and preserve it securely as US law requires.
- **An intimate image of you, shared without your consent.** Email us with "Intimate image" in the subject. Say:
  - that you are the person shown, or that you're acting for them
  - that your report is made in good faith and is true to the best of your knowledge
  - which recipe or photo it is
  - how we can contact you

  We take it down, with any identical copies we find, within 48 hours of your report.
- **Copyright.** If a recipe or photo copies your work, use the [Copyright page](copyright.html). It explains what a copyright notice needs.
- **Terrorist or extremist material.** In the UK you can also report it at [gov.uk/report-terrorism](https://www.gov.uk/report-terrorism).

## What happens next

1. **We confirm we've received it.** We reply to your email, and we aim to within 24 hours.
2. **A person reviews it.** We look at the content against the law you named and against our [community guidelines](guidelines.html). We aim to review reports within 24 hours, and an intimate image is taken down within 48 hours at most. We may ask you for more detail. Automated tools don't decide reports. A person does.
3. **We decide and act.** If the content is illegal, or breaks the community guidelines, we take it down for everyone. We may also block the cook, so that nothing they've shared is shown and they can't share again. We report serious crimes to the authorities.
4. **We tell you what we decided**, and why, by reply. If you disagree, we tell you how to challenge it. We don't tell the cook who reported them.

**If you disagree** with our decision, reply to it with "Appeal" in the subject. A person looks at it again and tells you the outcome. The [Terms of Use](terms.html) explain how we moderate Explore, and your other options.

## What we do with your report

Your email stays in our inbox, which is a Gmail account. It is never copied into Explore or iCloud. We keep reports of illegal content for three years, so we can account for what we did, and then delete them. The [privacy policy](privacy.html) has the details.

## For authorities

Our single point of contact under the EU Digital Services Act, for EU member states' authorities, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services, is **[rodrigobayapps@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigobayapps@gmail.com)**. You can write in English. We have not appointed a legal representative in the EU. Removal orders under Regulation (EU) 2021/784 can be sent to this address.
