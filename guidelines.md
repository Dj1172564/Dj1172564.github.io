# Recimama Community Guidelines

*Last updated: 29 September 2026*

Explore is where people share recipes with everyone who uses Recimama. These rules keep it a place worth browsing. You agree to them before you first share a recipe, and again whenever they change. They apply to everything you publish: the title, description, ingredients, steps, tags, photo, your name and your note. The [Terms of Use](terms.html) say more about what sharing means and how we moderate Explore.

## Share only what's yours

- Share recipes **you wrote yourself, in your own words**, and photos **you took yourself**. Every time you share, the app asks you to confirm it: "I wrote this recipe myself and took this photo. It isn't copied from a website, book, app, video or another person." For a recipe Recimama invented for you, it asks you to confirm that instead: "Recimama invented this recipe for me, and I took this photo."
- **Credit isn't permission.** Don't share anything copied from a cookbook, a magazine, a website, an app, a video or another person, even with the source named or a few words changed. If someone else's recipe inspired yours, write the method in your own words and use your own photo. Saying "Adapted from…" in your note is a courtesy. It doesn't make copying allowed.
- Recimama won't let you publish a recipe you imported from a website, read from a photographed page, added from Explore, received from someone else, or that came with the app. It also refuses pasted text that looks copied: text with a web address, ©, "All rights reserved", "Jump to recipe", "Print recipe", "Nutrition Facts" or "mealime" in it. The app tells you why.
- Sometimes the app can't tell that a camera took your photo (a screenshot, for example). Then it asks whether you took it. Only say yes if you did.
- Don't use someone else's name, logo or brand as if it were yours.

## Recipes written with AI

- You can share a recipe Recimama invented for you with Apple Intelligence. On Explore it's labelled **"Invented with Apple Intelligence"**, with a reminder to check the food, because Recimama wrote it and can't see or taste anything. Don't claim in your note that you created it.
- A recipe written by any other AI tool isn't one you wrote. Don't share it as your own.

## Keep it about food, and keep it decent

There is **no tolerance** for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't share:

- anything hateful, or anything that attacks or demeans people for who they are
- sexual content or nudity, including any intimate image of a person shared without their consent
- anything that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child. We report it to the authorities.
- threats, harassment or bullying, including in your name or note
- violent, graphic or shocking images
- anything that promotes eating disorders or extreme restriction. That means content presenting starving, purging, extreme fasting or dangerously fast weight loss as a goal, "pro-ana" or "thinspiration" content, and very-low-calorie challenges.
- anything illegal or dangerous, including recipes presented as safe that aren't
- spam, advertising, links or promotions, or anything that isn't a recipe
- other people's personal information

## Be careful with safety claims

- Don't say a recipe is safe for an allergy or a medical condition. Cooks reading it can't check your ingredients, your kitchen or your packaging.
- People will follow your recipe in their own kitchens. Give real cooking times and temperatures, and say what to check, for example that chicken is cooked through.

## If you see something wrong

Open the recipe, or long-press it in Explore, and tap **Report**. Choose a reason and, if you like, add a comment. The reasons include **illegal content**, where you tell us which law, and **an intimate image of you shared without your consent**. If you'd like a reply, the app offers to send your report by email too. You can also **Block** the cook, which hides everything they share from you and lets us know. Or **Hide** a single recipe.

If reporting in the app doesn't work, or you don't have the app, email **[rodrigobayapps@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigobayapps@gmail.com)** with the recipe's title and the cook's name. To report something illegal, see [Report illegal content](report.html). For a copyright complaint, see the [Copyright page](copyright.html).

## What happens next

- **Anyone can report a recipe. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.** An intimate image shared without consent is taken down within 48 hours at most.
- A recipe that breaks these guidelines or the law is **taken down** for everyone.
- A cook who breaks them seriously or repeatedly is **blocked from Explore**. Everything they have shared disappears for everyone, and the app won't let them share again. Something clearly illegal, hateful or sexual can get a cook blocked the first time. Two valid copyright notices get a cook blocked, and all their shared recipes removed.
- Recimama checks words automatically when you publish and when recipes are shown. That check is a safety net, not permission: something that gets past it can still be taken down.
- If you think we got it wrong, email [rodrigobayapps@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigobayapps@gmail.com) and we'll look again.

## Your recipes stay yours

Sharing a recipe lets everyone who uses Recimama read it and add a copy to their own library. The [Terms of Use](terms.html) explain the permission you give. You can take it down at any time from **Explore > More > Recipes you shared**. Copies people have already added stay in their libraries.

How we handle what you publish is explained in the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
