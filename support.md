# Recimama Support

Questions, problems, or something that doesn't look right? Email **[rodrigobayapps@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigobayapps@gmail.com)**. Recimama is made by one person, David Rodriguez, and every email is read. You should hear back within two working days.

It helps to include:
- what you were doing when it went wrong, and what you expected;
- your iPhone, iPad or Mac model and its system version (**Settings › General › About** on an iPhone or iPad);
- a screenshot, if you can.

## Reporting a recipe on Explore

Open the recipe and tap **⋯ › Report this recipe…**, or press and hold it in the Explore list and choose **Report…**. Pick a reason and, if you like, add a line. We aim to review reports within 24 hours. You can also block a cook, which hides everything they share: **⋯ › Block** on any of their recipes.

If reporting doesn't work, the app offers to send the report by email instead, or you can write to the address above. The app also offers the email if you'd like a reply. If you report an intimate image of yourself, please send that email too: it's written for you, with the statements UK law asks of such a report, and it's how we can contact you.

- To report something illegal, with or without the app: [Report illegal content](report.html).
- If a recipe or photo on Explore copies your work: [Copyright](copyright.html).

## Taking down something you published

In the app: **Settings › About › Remove something I published**. You can also open the recipe on Explore and take it down from there. If you no longer have the device you published from, email us and we'll remove it.

## Recimama Pro: cancelling and refunds

Apple sells Recimama Pro and takes every payment, so cancelling and refunds go through your Apple Account.

- **To cancel on an iPhone or iPad:** **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions**, then Recimama Pro — the words the Recimama Pro screen uses.
- **To cancel on a Mac:** **App Store > [your name] > Subscriptions**.
- **Or, on any of them:** in Recimama, **Settings › Purchases › Manage Subscription**.
- **The 24-hour rule.** A subscription renews automatically at the price shown until you cancel; to stop the next renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. If the price ever changes, Apple tells you first. The same goes for a free trial: the Recimama Pro screen gives the day it ends and says "Cancel at least 24 hours before then and you won't be charged." After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **Refunds** are Apple's to give, and we can't issue them ourselves. Ask Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), or read [Apple's refund help](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). If you're in the EU or the UK, cancelling a new purchase within 14 days also goes through Apple. If Apple refunds you, Pro ends.
- **Something wrong with Pro?** If a purchase hasn't unlocked, try **Settings › Purchases › Restore Purchases** first. If that doesn't fix it, email **[rodrigobayapps@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigobayapps@gmail.com)**. We can't see your payment, but we can help with the app.

If Pro ends, nothing you made with it is deleted.

## Ads

The free version shows a few ads from Google while you browse, never while you cook. This version has no full-screen, app-open, video or rewarded ads. You won't see any if you have ever paid for Recimama Pro, or while you're in a free trial or share Pro through Family Sharing. On a Mac there are no ads. To report an ad, tap the flag beside it. What Google receives is in the [privacy policy](privacy.html), section 11.

## Your recipes and iCloud

Your library is stored on your device and, if **Settings › Storage › Sync with iCloud** is on, in your own private iCloud, which only you can see. A new iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account gets your recipes back; a large library can take a few minutes to arrive.

To keep a copy of your own: **Settings › Backup › Export a backup**, and **Restore from a backup** to bring it back.
